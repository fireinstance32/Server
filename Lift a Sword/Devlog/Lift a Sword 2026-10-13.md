# Lift a Sword: 2026-10-13 / 14

Back to [[Lift a Sword]].

- **Picked the game:** Lift a Sword, the Lift a Weight / Lift a Hammer concept where you pull a sword out of the stone.
- **Map:** 15 zone strips (140 × 150) on a sea, with biome banks decorated from the nature pack.
- **Swords:** 15 built. The first pass was too blocky, so they were smoothed and given baked textures (approved). Turned so the blade faces the player, and seated so the handle and some bare blade show.
- **Pedestals:** 15 themed, with a trial splinter collar on the wooden stump (subtle, with mixed sizes).
- **Eggs:** 15 themed eggs plus a marble stand, placed on the egg stairs with a glowing ring per egg. The price labels sit above the eggs.
- **Signs:** 15 themed signs placed in the zones, with the "💪 recommended" text on each board.
- **Pets:** the chibi sample was rejected, and pets are on hold.
- **Props:** the spin wheel and daily rewards will be UI. Other props are dropped.
- **Portal + group chest:** built to your references. The chest was rebuilt way bigger and more detailed, and both are exported with flat colours (no textures).
- **Notes:** moved into this vault folder, and started [[3D Modelling Preferences]].
