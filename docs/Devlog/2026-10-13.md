# 2026-10-13

- **Pivot: +1 Frog Hop → rock lifting.** Same Stone Skipping loop: lift your rock for Strength, throw it down the field for Wins. Pets, eggs, rebirth, potions, the Shop and all the UI stay. Plan, naming table and stages in [[Rock Pivot]].
- **Stage 1 – words:** every on-screen Hops / Frog / Pond / Hop / River string changed: Hops → **Strength**, frogs → **rocks** (Pebble, Mossy Stone, Clay Rock … Meteorite, Phoenix Stone; world rocks like Moon Rock, Magma Rock, Rock Candy), ponds → **gyms** (Starter, Iron, Crystal, Royal, Golden, Rainbow, Cosmic), HOP button → **THROW**, Auto Hop → **Auto Lift**, Hop Zone → **Throw Zone**, Lily Valley → **Stone Valley**, frog-named pets / titles / codes (codes now WELCOME, STRONG, ROCKON). Potion chips read their stat through `Potions.StatWord`. Code names (`data.hops`, `HopService`, `Config/Frogs`, `Ponds`) are unchanged so saves keep working.
- **Stage 2 – gameplay (first pass):** frogs are now rocks in code too: `Models.rock` (new `Models/Rock.luau`: a few rotated blocks make a faceted rock, same shape per rock id; glowing rocks get neon flecks and a light). It's used for the held rock, the thrown rock (it tumbles in flight), podiums and the Rocks tab. Gym training is now a lift rep (`LiftAnimation`: the arm presses the rock overhead, +Strength pops at the top) instead of a frog hopping pads. Rock colours changed to stone colours; Bullfrog → Boulder. Not tested in Studio yet (Studio wasn't connected).
- **Title:** *+1 Lift Rock For Pets*.
- **Prices:** one middle ladder for every Robux item — small 19–59, most passes/packs 79–129, big 199–449 (was a mix of 5 to 540 copied from the reference). Full list in [[Monetization Setup]] (rewritten with the new names and every Shop product).
- **Starter Pack:** 49 (was 249), now with an exclusive pet, the **Rainbow Unicat** (Legendary, ×25: cat + unicorn horn + rainbow mane + neon rainbow feather wings, its own face `face_unicat.png`). The HUD Starter Pack is now a picture put together in code like the reference: Strength potion, bolt and rebirth icons fanned behind the Unicat, a slow golden sunburst, idle bob; hidden once bought. `pets.png` re-rendered (45 pets) and re-uploaded (new id in `UiImages`).
- **Lift / toss fix:** your avatar uses Roblox's upgraded joints (the shoulder is an `AnimationConstraint`, not a `Motor6D`), so the procedural arm moves did nothing. New `Controllers/Shoulder.luau` adds the arm angle on top of the Animator every frame on either joint type; tested in Studio: the hand goes from waist to over the head each lift.
- **Blender live (MCP):** Claude now drives Blender directly through the Blender MCP server.
- **Stage 4 – rock models:** `tools/blender/build_rocks.py` builds **19 rock shapes + a Crown**, high quality (dense meshes shaped with noise, chiselled facets with hard edges, craters, metaball blobs, smooth "coats" for moss/frosting/ooze/gold/lava). Each one is different: Pebble, River, Mossy, Rock, Boulder, Nugget, Lava, Meteor, Moon, Candy (on a stick), Gummy (a gummy bear), Frosted (cupcake + cherry), Mushroom, Toxic, Rune, Royal (gold inlays), Comet (glowing tail), Galaxy (ringed orb), Phoenix (flame feathers). Exported to `assets/models/Rocks.fbx` (+ one file per shape in `assets/models/rocks/`). ![[Rocks Preview.png]]
- `Config/RockLooks` picks a shape for every rock in all 5 worlds and crowns the 4 Kings (unit test checks it). `Models.rock` uses the shape from `Assets.Rocks`, falls back to the plain `Rock` shape, then to the part-built rock; `Models/Assets` colours parts by role (`<Shape>_<Role>`), Glow parts are Neon, the hidden `Root` is the pivot.
- **Rock pictures:** `tools/blender/render_rocks.py` renders every rock from the same models; `frogs.png` repacked and uploaded (new id in `UiImages`), so the Rocks tab and Daily Day 7 show rocks now.
- **Ore ladder:** Stone Valley's 10 rocks are now Pebble → Stone → Coal → Copper → Iron → Silver → Gold → Emerald → Ruby → Diamond, with 4 new shapes (Ore nuggets, OreRich veins, GemOre pockets, GemCluster). The pebble is now a real pebble (lopsided, water-worn, a quartz vein, speckles), not an oval.
- **Reward rocks:** the old Stone Valley uniques are earned, not bought: codes **MOSSY** (Mossy Stone), **SLIME** (Toxic Rock), **ROYAL** (Royal Gem); Level 20 quest (River Stone); Rebirth quest (Shadow Rock); Daily Day 4 (Gold Nugget); the 45 min gift (Boulder). They stay off the podiums and the tooltip says where they come from.
- **The lift:** one lift animation, done properly: squat with the rock at the shoulder → explosive drive → press overhead with straight arms, the other arm flexes, chest up → relax. `Controllers/Pose` (replaces `Shoulder`) poses the whole body (shoulders, elbows, hips, knees, waist, neck) on top of the Animator, R15 or R6. Tested in Studio.
- **Lift effects** (`Effects/Lift`): squat = dust at the feet + motes rushing into the rock; lockout = flash, shockwave rings, sparks, stars, a light pop, camera punch, ground ring. Scales with the rock's strength.
- **Rock auras** (`Effects/RockAura`): every rock in a hand or on a podium gets glints plus its shape's own style (lava embers, phoenix flames, toxic bubbles, rune wisps, frost, spores, sweets, shine, gem prisms, galaxy stars); strong rocks get orbiting comets and the strongest a halo. `Effects/Kit` holds the shared pieces.
- **Rock pictures** re-rendered (35 rocks) and uploaded.
- **Props:** `tools/blender/build_props.py` builds a lifting platform, dumbbell, kettlebell, bench, loaded squat rack, plate tree, weight pile, a stone Podium with gold trim and a glow ring, a stone PortalArch with a glowing rune, and a Trophy. Gyms, rock podiums and portals use them once imported (`WorldBuilder/GymKit`, `Parts.prop`), with the old parts kept as collision and fallback. ![[Props Preview.png]]
- **Models folder:** everything is in `assets/models/` by kind: `Rocks/` (+ `Shapes/`), `Props/` (+ `Shapes/`), `Pets/`, `Eggs/`, `Legacy/`; index in `assets/models/README.md`.
- Auto-import into Studio isn't possible (scripts can't save meshes into the place yet), so you do Home → Import 3D once for `Rocks/Rocks.fbx` and `Props/Props.fbx`.
- Next: stage 3 (rock icon, frogs.png picture sheet → rock pictures), stage 4 (Blender rocks, gyms, the field map, real lift/throw animations).
- **Fix after import:** the rocks came in grey because `Models/Assets` had no colour rule for the new **Ore** and **Gem** parts; added (Ore = metal in the rock's colour, Gem = glass). The metal ores (Coal → Gold) now sit in a dark stone of their own colour (`Shade`) instead of grey, so they read as copper / iron / gold at a glance (the imported parts were renamed in Studio, no re-import needed). Podium rocks are 50% bigger.
- **Strength multiplier tiers:** the +2x Strength pass is now a ladder like the reference's Skill Multiplier: x2 for 5, x4 for 19, x8 for 49, x16 for 129 (the highest owned counts; the HUD and Shop cards offer the next tier). Unit-tested.
