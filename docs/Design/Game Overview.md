# Game Overview

**Pitch:** A frog-hopping take on [[Stone Skipping Reference|+1 Stone Skipping]]. Easy for young kids to read at a glance: frog jumps, numbers go up.

**Audience:** 7–13, mobile-first. One big button, bright colours, chunky text.

**Why frogs:** no "+1" frog game found in the [[Competitor Scan]]; animals have strong kid appeal; frogs double as collectibles (frog "skins" are the upgrade path, like stones).

## Pillars
1. **One button** – HOP does the right thing depending on where you stand.
2. **Satisfying chain** – every pad splashes and pops a number.
3. **Always something to buy** – next frog, next egg, next rebirth.
4. **Clip-worthy** – camera follows the frog; big zone banner on landing.
